        Methodology

        How the match is calculated

        Each answer becomes a weight. We compare those priorities with a maintained capability profile for each tool, then calculate a weighted fit score. A high score means the product aligns well with the things you rated most highly.

        The profiles cover Microsoft 365 and Google fit, writing and long documents, research, numbers, building, images, audio and video, voice, ease of use, cost, heavy use, security, teamwork, automation and accuracy. They are directional—not laboratory benchmarks—and should be reviewed as products change.
