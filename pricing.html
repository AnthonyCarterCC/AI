Usage matters too

Some tools use their allowance faster than others.

AI is measured in tokens, small chunks of text. Heavier tools think longer and use more of them, so the cheapest seat is not always the cheapest tool. Here is roughly how quickly each one uses its allowance in everyday business work.

  • Claude Moderate

    Uses the most tokens of the five for similar work. Plan for heavy users to run out.

  • ChatGPT Moderate

    Generous usage for the price.

  • Gemini Low

    Generous usage for the price.

  • Copilot Low

    Includes OpenAI and Claude models, but with limited functionality. Quicker and deeper thinking modes are available without running out. Heavier jobs use Copilot Credits, billed on top.

  • Grok Very low

    Very low token use. Most people will not reach the limit.
