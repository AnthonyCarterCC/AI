      Methodology

      How the match is calculated

      Each answer becomes a weight. We compare those priorities with a maintained capability profile for each assistant, then calculate a weighted fit score. A high score means the product aligns well with the things you rated most highly.

      The profiles cover ecosystem fit, document work, research, coding, multimodal creation, live information, voice, ease of use, business controls and value. They are directional—not laboratory benchmarks—and should be reviewed as products change.

      Important caveats

      • Features vary by plan, region and account type.
      • AI outputs can be wrong. Verify important work.
      • For sensitive business data, check your organisation’s policy and the vendor’s current terms.
